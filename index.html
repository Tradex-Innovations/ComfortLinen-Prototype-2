03 / A LIGHTER FOOTPRINT

Good for your linen.
Thoughtful for
our world.

A little more care makes a difference.
It’s woven into the way we work.

Water has more than one life in our laundry. Recycling water helps us make better use of a precious resource, wash after wash.

Our own electricity generation and heat recovery help us use energy more thoughtfully, bringing care for the environment into everyday operations.

Thoughtful laundering and careful finishing keep quality linen in circulation. We care for the details, giving each piece a fresh start.

04 / OUR STORY

DIFFERENT SHORES. THE SAME CARE.

From Galle.
With purpose.

A journey shaped by nature.

Our co-founder, Mr. Gayanath Nanayakkara, began his journey in Galle, Sri Lanka. He brought that connection to nature to a new chapter in the UK, helping build ComfortLinen in Axminster.

Today, the same outlook guides our work: dependable care for our customers and a more considered use of resources.
